# North star: Hemi, sports advisor

**Set 25 Nov 2026. Every agent, session, résumé, deck, demo and project follows this.**

## The goal
Become a **consultant / advisor to the sports industry**: teams, leagues, sports organizations, sports tech, sports brands. Start as an **advisory analyst / business analyst**, grow into a consultant in 3–5 years. Stay on the **business side**: technically strong enough to do the analysis, but valued for the business problem, the logic and the recommendation. Role model: a consultant career like those at Accenture.

## The mindset (how every piece of work is framed)
An analyst's job is to make a **recommendation people agree with because the logic is clear**. Every project, slide, résumé bullet and demo follows the same chain:

1. **Decision:** what business question is someone trying to answer? (Whom to sign? Where to spend? Which message works?)
2. **Data:** what evidence answers it, and why that data?
3. **Logic:** what the analysis shows, in plain words, with the key number.
4. **Recommendation:** what to do, stated as an action.
5. **Result / impact:** what changes if they do it (money, wins, fans, time).

Technical detail (model names, code) supports the story; it never leads it.

## Job search priorities
- **Roles, in order:** advisory / consulting analyst → business analyst → data / analytics analyst, in sports first, then any industry with a strong consulting path. Internships **and** full-time (graduates 05/2027).
- **Where:** major US cities first (NYC, Boston, Chicago, LA, SF Bay, DC, Atlanta, Dallas, Philadelphia, Seattle, Denver). Smaller towns if the role or pay is clearly better. **Money matters.** A Northeast location is a bonus.
- **Employers:** sports teams, leagues and orgs; sports consultancies and agencies; consulting firms with sports, media & entertainment practices; sports tech and brands.

## Skills to build (3–5 years)
- **Communication:** structured storytelling (answer first, then support), executive summaries, slide craft, presenting to non-technical people.
- **Business:** sports business models (media rights, ticketing, sponsorship, betting, player economics), financial basics, market sizing, case interviews.
- **AI-enabled analysis:** use AI to research, analyze and draft faster, always with human review.
- **Analytics core:** SQL, Excel, Tableau/Power BI, Python, experimentation.

## How outputs should look
- **Résumés:** advisory style by default (`style: advisory` in Job Hunter); one page; consultant-voice bullets.
- **Decks:** answer first (title = the recommendation), one idea per slide, evidence under it, next steps at the end.
- **Projects / GitHub / website:** open with the business question and the recommendation; methods come after.
- **Knowledge:** daily AI + sports-business news (US, India, European soccer) via the AI News agent.
